Model Trains

This is the Git Hub Pages web page for my Scarm repository. I am relearning HTML and CSS along with Git and Git Hub.

I am a controls engineer by trade and I have held a passion for model trains ever since I was a child. I can remember getting shocked by the vintage Lionel O Scale train that ran around under the Christmas tree.

When I started working as a teenager, I spent a lot of time in my local train shop. It was my escape from home, but I didn't have space to set up a layout and I would just dream. In the intervening years, both of my parents have passed and now I have a house with a basement that is all mine.

The model railroading hobby has come a long way since the 1980s and I am excited to see 3D printing and computer control. I am also excited to see layout editors such as Scarm that facilitate a vision and plan before ever spending any money or even building benchwork.

I am planning to build my own DCC system using Arduino, RaspberryPi, MQTT, and Ignition Maker Edition. I will also be playing with JMRI/CMRI and learning more about railroad operations with RFID tags. This is the beginning of my journey and I will be using this page to chronicle every step.

At some point I will also get started with videos on YouTube.

To start, I am working through learning Scarm (Simple Computer Aided Railway Modeller) and using Git Hub to keep up with different layout ideas.

One of my favorite sources of learning and inspiration is DIY and Digital Railroad. I love Jimmy's explanations and he has given me much to think about.
